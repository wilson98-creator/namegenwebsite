What do half-orc names sound like?

Half-orc names lean into short syllables - often just one or two. They keep the strong consonants of orcish but rarely have the guttural, rolled-R finish, which makes them easy to pronounce in a human court. This free half-orc name generator (and random name generator, name creator, and random name maker all in one) draws from a curated set of prefixes, middles, and suffixes tuned to that culture.
