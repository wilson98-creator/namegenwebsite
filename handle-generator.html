How to pick a great social media username

A good social media handle does three things: it tells people what your content is, it sticks in their head, and it stays available across platforms. This handle generator combines 14 niche categories with 8 vibes to produce uncommon combinations that are likely to be available.

The names use the patterns real creators actually pick: first name + niche (e.g. cookingwithsam), name + activity (e.g. chef.aria), mood + object (e.g. softbloom), the/hey/its/by/get/try + name (e.g. heymia), and name + small meaningful number (e.g. liv_04). No random combat syllables, no fantasy words.

Length rules by platform

  • X / Twitter: 15 characters max. Go with Short or Medium.
  • TikTok: 24 characters max. Short or Medium works best.
  • Instagram: 30 characters max, allows underscores and periods. Medium is the sweet spot.
  • Twitch: 25 characters max, lowercase only. Short or Medium.
  • YouTube: 100 characters max, allows spaces and capitalisation. Long is fine here.
  • Discord: 32 characters max for the username. Short or Medium.
  • GitHub: 39 characters max, allows hyphens. Medium or Long.

Frequently asked questions

How do I come up with a good social media username?

Start with your first name or nickname. Pick your niche (fashion, beauty, fitness, food, travel, art, music, photography, tech, gaming, comedy, education, lifestyle, or business). Combine the two with a common pattern: name + activity (cookingwithsam), name + niche word (miaskincare), mood + object (softbloom), or a small meaningful number (liv_04). Keep it under 15 characters for X, 24 for TikTok, 30 for Instagram.

How do I check if a username is available?

For Instagram, TikTok, X, and Twitch, paste your generated handle into the platform's sign-up or search. For YouTube, the handle is the same as your channel name, so check on YouTube. For Discord, the username is separate from the display name.

Can I use these names commercially?

Yes - all generated names are provided for creative use. Note that the actual availability on each platform is determined by the platform's sign-up system, so always verify before launching your account or brand.

Are these handles likely to be available?

We generate uncommon combinations of first names, activity verbs, niche keywords, mood adjectives, and aesthetic objects, so most suggestions are likely available. We can't check every platform in real time, so the names show a 'likely available' indicator based on uniqueness, not a live check.

What's the difference between a handle and a display name?

The handle is your unique @username (no spaces, used in URLs and mentions). The display name is the readable name shown next to your posts. You usually set both, and they can be different versions of the same idea.

Should my Instagram handle match my TikTok handle?

Yes, ideally. A consistent handle across platforms makes it easier for fans to find you everywhere. Use the Surprise me button until you find one you like, then register it on every platform you use.

What's a good username for a private/personal account vs a public/creator account?

For a personal account, lean into your own name or a nickname (e.g. maya.r or max_22). For a public creator account, lean into your niche + vibe so the handle communicates your content at a glance (e.g. cookingwithsam for a food channel).
