What do dragon names sound like?

Dragon names lean into ancient and weighty. They open with hard consonants and close with heavy endings - they sound older than the language they live in. This free dragon name generator (and random name generator, name creator, and random name maker all in one) draws from a curated set of prefixes, middles, and suffixes tuned to that culture. Common opening sounds include Vyr, Az, Mor, Bax, Ka, and Vh; common endings include -ax, -ion, -morg, -reth, -akir, and -azar.
