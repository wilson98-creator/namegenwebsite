What is NameSwift?

NameSwift is a free fantasy and gaming name generator built for writers, Dungeon Masters, and gamers. Every page is a focused generator for a single name type - from elf names to gamertags - so you land on the right tool in a single tap.

Each generator draws from a curated bank of prefixes, middles, and suffixes tuned to that culture, producing tens of thousands of unique combinations. Nothing is sent to a server; all generation runs in your browser, so names appear instantly and the page stays private.

Why NameSwift beats the old name generator sites

  • Mobile-first and fast. No heavy pages, no popups, no required signup.
  • Dark mode that actually looks good. Designed for late-night DM sessions.
  • One-click copy and a session-only Saved names list, so you never lose a name you liked.
  • Full character generator on every fantasy page, with a short backstory and traits you can share.
  • Lore-friendly syllable banks. Orc names sound orcish. Elven names sound elven.

How to use it

  1. Pick a generator from the grid above, or use search to jump straight to one.
  2. Hit Generate. A fresh batch of names appears in under a second.
  3. Tap the copy icon to grab a name, or the heart to save it for later in this session.
  4. Need a full character? Tap Generate a full character on any fantasy page.
