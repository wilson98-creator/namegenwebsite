How to pick a good gaming name

A great gamertag has four traits: it's short (under 12 characters reads well in any UI), memorable (one sharp word is better than three forgettable ones), pronounceable (your squad needs to be able to call it out in clutch), and genre-appropriate (a name that fits the game you're playing most). Avoid numbers and underscores when you can - they date your account and look like a placeholder.

How to change your gamertag in 2026

Every major platform lets you change your gamertag, though the rules vary:

  • Steam: Free once, then $5 per change. Profile names are separate from your account name.
  • Xbox: First change is free, then $9.99 per change. Limited to one change per 30 days.
  • PlayStation: First change free on PSN, then $4.99 per change. PSN IDs are unique forever - if someone has the name you want, even inactive, you can't take it.
  • Epic Games: Free, can change every 2 weeks.
  • Riot (Valorant, LoL): Free, can change occasionally, must include at least one letter and one number.
  • Battle.net: Free, can change every 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

What's a good gamertag?

Short, memorable, pronounceable, and genre-appropriate. Aim for under 12 characters with one sharp word. Avoid numbers and underscores when possible.

How do I come up with a cool gamertag?

Pick a vibe (cool, funny, edgy, anime) and a genre (fantasy, sci-fi, modern, horror). Combine one short word from each. Our gamertag generator does this for you - it produces 10 unique combinations in under a second.

Can I use the same gamertag on every platform?

Often, but not always. Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, and Riot all have their own name spaces. Check availability on each platform after you find one you like.

Should my gamertag match my Twitch / YouTube name?

Ideally yes - consistency makes it easier for fans to find you. Secure the name on every platform you stream on, even if you don't use them all yet. For real-name style handles, try our social handle generator which produces names people actually use on Twitch, YouTube, and Discord.

Can I change my gamertag later?

Most platforms let you change, usually for a small fee (Xbox $9.99, PSN $4.99, Steam $5). So don't stress too much - but picking a good one upfront saves money.
