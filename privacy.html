Privacy Policy

Short version: we do not collect personal data, do not save your generated names, and do not run third-party analytics by default. Read on for the full policy.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal data. We do not ask for your name, email, or any other identifying information to use the site.
  • No saved names. Generated names and your "Saved names" list live in memory only, for the duration of your session. They are cleared the moment you close the tab.
  • No analytics by default. We do not embed Google Analytics, Plausible, or any other third-party tracker on the core site. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first.
  • No fingerprinting. We do not fingerprint your browser or device.

Data we do collect (the minimum)

  • Theme preference. We save a single localStorage entry: nf-theme = dark or light. This is purely so the site remembers your choice between visits. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.
  • Server logs. If we host the site, the host's server may record your IP address, user agent, and the pages you request, in the same way every other website does. This is used for security, abuse prevention, and aggregate traffic counts. Logs are rotated regularly.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies on the core site. The single localStorage entry (nf-theme) is not a cookie and is not used for tracking.

Third parties

We do not embed third-party trackers, fonts, or scripts on the core site. If we add Google AdSense later, Google will set its own cookies and may collect data according to its own privacy policy. We will update this page when AdSense is added, and the AdSense placements will be marked clearly with empty divs that you can choose to fill or leave empty.

Children's privacy

The site is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Because we do not collect personal data in the first place, there is no children's data to delete or export.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we will update this page and bump the date below. If you have a question about a change, reach out via the contact page.
