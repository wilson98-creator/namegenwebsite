What do witch names sound like?

Witch names lean into old-world, herb-scented, and quietly powerful. They feel less evil and more "wise woman" than infernal names. This free witch name generator (and random name generator, name creator, and random name maker all in one) draws from a curated set of prefixes, middles, and suffixes tuned to that culture. Common opening sounds include Mor, Hel, Elsb, and other warm old-world openings; common endings include -ga, -an, -eth, -ild, -en, and -yn.
