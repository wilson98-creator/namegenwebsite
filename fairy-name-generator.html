What do fairy names sound like?

Fairy names lean into delicate, often two or three syllables, with bell-like or leaf-like endings. They feel like a small laugh in a forest clearing. This free fairy name generator (and random name generator, name creator, and random name maker all in one) draws from a curated set of prefixes, middles, and suffixes tuned to that culture. Common opening sounds include Lu, Mi, Ch, Sylv, Fae, and Ti; common endings include -bell, -wen, -lily, -wing, -lith, and -sparrow.
