Contact NameSwift

Got feedback, a generator request, or a generated name that needs flagging? Send a note - we read every message.

Get in touch

The fastest way to reach the team is by email. We read every message and try to respond within a few days.

What to write about

  • Generated name matches a real person or trademark. Send the page, the name, and a link to the real person or mark. We will adjust the banks if the match is too close.
  • Generator request. Tell us the name type, the genre, and a couple of example names you like. We are always looking for the next batch.
  • Bug or accessibility issue. Send the page, the device, and the browser. We will fix it as soon as we can.
  • General feedback. Anything else - what you love, what you would change, what is missing.

Response time

We aim to respond within 3-5 working days. Bug reports usually get a fix within a week. Generator requests get added to the roadmap and shipped as soon as the syllable banks are ready.
