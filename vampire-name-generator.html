What do vampire names sound like?

Vampire names lean into gothic, old-world, and weighty. They open with sharp or aristocratic sounds and close with equally old endings. This free vampire name generator (and random name generator, name creator, and random name maker all in one) draws from a curated set of prefixes, middles, and suffixes tuned to that culture. Common opening sounds include Vlad, Drac, Lest, Nos, Mina, and Elsb; common endings include -escu, -feratu, -ius, -ia, -eus, and -anda.
