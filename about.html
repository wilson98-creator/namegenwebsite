About NameSwift

A fast, mobile-first fantasy and gaming name generator built for writers, D&D players, and gamers. Every name is generated in your browser - no backend, no signup, no waiting.

What is NameSwift?

NameSwift is a curated collection of name generators for fantasy races, dark fantasy creatures, and gaming handles. Every page on the site is a focused generator for a single name type - from elf names to gamertags - so you land on the right tool in a single tap.

Who is it for?

  • Writers who need a quick character name for a novel, short story, or roleplaying game.
  • D&D players and DMs who need a name for an NPC, a tavern, or a whole warband.
  • Gamers who want a fresh gamertag, Riot ID, or username for a new platform.
  • Worldbuilders who want dozens of unique names for a culture in a single click.
  • Anyone who has ever been stuck on what to call their character.

How does it work?

Each generator uses a curated bank of prefixes, middles, and suffixes tuned to a specific culture or genre. The generator randomly combines them to produce a believable name, with a small chance of pulling from a list of hand-picked "classic" names for that type. All generation runs locally in your browser - nothing is sent to a server, nothing is logged, nothing is stored between sessions except the dark/light theme preference.

How is it different from the old name generator sites?

  • Mobile-first and fast. No heavy pages, no popups, no required signup.
  • Dark mode that actually looks good. Designed for late-night DM sessions.
  • One-click copy and a session-only Saved names list, so you never lose a name you liked.
  • Full character generator on every fantasy page, with a short backstory and traits you can share.
  • Lore-friendly syllable banks. Orc names sound orcish. Elven names sound elven.

What's next?

More generators are on the way. If you have a request - a specific race, a specific game, a specific vibe - reach out via the contact page and we will consider it for the next batch.

Built with care

NameSwift is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is no build step, no framework, and no third-party scripts that track you. The site is designed to load fast on a phone on a slow connection, and to keep working even when your network drops out mid-generation.

Disclaimer
